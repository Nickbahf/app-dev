## 👥 Main Characters

1. **Columbus** – Played by *Jesse Eisenberg*
   - A nerdy college student who is afraid of almost everything.
2. **Tallahassee** – Played by *Woody Harrelson*
   - A fearless man who loves guns and Twinkies.
3. **Wichita** – Played by *Emma Stone*
   - A clever and resourceful survivor.
4. **Little Rock** – Played by *Abigail Breslin*
   - Wichita's younger sister.

---

## 📖 Movie Synopsis

The story follows Columbus, a nerdy college student who survives a zombie apocalypse because he follows strict survival rules.

He meets Tallahassee, a fearless man who loves Twinkies. Along their journey, they encounter two sisters, Wichita and Little Rock.

Together, they face dangerous zombies, unexpected challenges, and hilarious situations.

> "In Zombieland, surviving is important, but having fun while surviving is even better!"
